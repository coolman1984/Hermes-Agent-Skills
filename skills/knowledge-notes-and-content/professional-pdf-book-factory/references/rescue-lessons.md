# PDF Book Rescue Lessons

## Mohamed Correction

If Mohamed says the full-book output looks worse than the approved sample, stop treating the previous PDF as acceptable and rebuild from the approved sample design language.

## Rules

1. Never convert extracted PDF text into one flowing manuscript without restoring structure first.
2. Do not collapse tables into random paragraphs.
3. Do not force every source page into a new visual section if that creates blank or weak pages.
4. Use the approved sample as the visual contract when Mohamed liked it.
5. Render a contact sheet and compare it against the sample before delivery.
6. If the contact sheet shows empty pages, broken flow, or a different design language, iterate before sending.
7. For urgent rescue, deliver a stable sample-style edition first, then offer deeper editorial restructuring later.
8. If Mohamed complains that pages are half empty, treat it as a broken reader promise, not a cosmetic spacing issue. Rebuild with natural book-style text flow, unified page color, no two-tone page backgrounds, medium-length explanatory paragraphs, numerical examples, mental images, and interview-ready answers.
9. Avoid locking every concept to its own forced page. Global professional books let content flow across pages so pages fill naturally without squeezing or leaving wasteful blank areas.
10. If a publishing-style critique says the book has confused identity, repetitive structure, weak proofreading, too many tables, and content below its promise, do a strategic rebuild: define one target reader, rewrite the promise honestly, remove inflated claims, vary chapter openings, reduce recurring interview boxes, add decision-oriented cases, and run automatic visible-text gates for Latin terms and known typo patterns.

## Verification

- qpdf check passes.
- veraPDF passes if PDF/A is requested.
- contact sheet reviewed.
- no embedded Latin terms inside Arabic lines unless intentionally standalone.
- no low-text accidental pages.
