# YouTube Geopolitical Economy Verification Notes

Use when a YouTube source makes broad geopolitical-economy claims that affect investing, energy, war, sanctions, shipping routes, sovereign wealth, AI infrastructure, or Mohamed's Egyptian equity workflow.

## Trigger

Apply this reference when the video is not just explanatory content, but proposes a causal map such as:

- war versus reconstruction incentives
- energy chokepoints such as the Strait of Hormuz
- sanctions, ceasefires, memoranda, or diplomatic deals
- Gulf capital, sovereign wealth funds, data centers, AI infrastructure, or defense contractors
- political donors, asset managers, or financial-industrial power structures

## Verification pattern

1. Extract the transcript first and separate the creator's claims from the creator's interpretation.
2. Treat the video as an interpretive source, not as primary evidence.
3. Verify hard claims with a source ladder:
   - official or institutional data for energy chokepoints and volumes
   - Reuters or comparable wire reporting for live diplomacy and ceasefire claims
   - reputable academic or institutional sources for intervention-history claims
   - reputable political reporting for donor and political-network claims
4. If direct Reuters pages are blocked by CAPTCHA or empty readable extraction, use search-result snippets only as low-confidence context, and label that clearly in the note.
5. Prefer a retrieved accessible source that quotes or summarizes the wire report over pretending the wire article was fully read.
6. For AI/data-center energy claims, do not rely on a blocked page. Either retrieve an accessible institutional page or explicitly label that research gap.
7. Do not over-promote the source into a project. Most of these videos become a decision lens inside investment/knowledge systems.

## Required provenance language in the final note

The note should explicitly distinguish:

- what the video said directly
- what external sources support
- what remains an interpretation or causal hypothesis
- what Mohamed should do with the lens

## Mohamed-specific classification

Default classification for geopolitical-economy videos:

- save as a lens
- attach to Egyptian equity risk review when relevant
- do not make a new project
- use the lens during major events affecting energy, dollar liquidity, shipping, Gulf capital, or market risk appetite

## Practical output

Add a concise idea-vault bridge only if the source yields a reusable decision lens, such as:

- who benefits from war?
- who benefits from stability?
- what chokepoint matters?
- what asset will be financed or privatized?
- what sectors or watchlist names are exposed?

The bridge should be a small reusable lens, not a duplicate summary of the video.
