# Research Enrichment Before Media Note

Use this reference whenever Mohamed sends a substantial link and expects a durable media note rather than a quick chat answer.

## Goal

Do not reduce the source to a summary. Convert it into a usable knowledge asset by combining:

1. The source's own claims.
2. The source's explicit references.
3. Independent context.
4. Skeptical critique.
5. Mohamed-specific application.

## The Five-Layer Model

### 1) Source Layer

Answer only from the original source:

- What did the author actually say?
- What examples did they use?
- What definitions did they introduce?
- What claims are central versus side remarks?
- What links, people, books, studies, companies, tools, or institutions were mentioned?

### 2) Context Layer

Research the missing background:

- Historical origin: how did this idea, market, tool, policy, or conflict emerge?
- Timeline: what key events changed the situation?
- Actors: who are the main players and what incentives do they have?
- Geography: where is the action concentrated?
- Institutions: what laws, agencies, companies, protocols, or platforms shape the topic?
- Philosophy: what worldview, ideology, or incentive structure sits underneath?

### 3) Skeptical Layer

Challenge the source:

- What did the author omit?
- Where could the framing be biased?
- What counterargument would a serious expert make?
- Are the numbers old, contested, or selectively chosen?
- Is the author mixing correlation with causation?
- Is the story emotionally compelling but analytically incomplete?

### 4) Mohamed Layer

Translate the topic into Mohamed's systems:

- Does it improve investment decision quality?
- Does it strengthen agent, memory, vault, or research systems?
- Does it create a product, service, content, or business opportunity?
- Does it belong in an existing project?
- Is it just interesting and should be parked?
- What is the smallest practical experiment?

### 5) Output Layer

Write the final note with clear provenance:

- "من المصدر مباشرة" for source claims.
- "من البحث الإضافي" for added context.
- "قراءة عملية لمحمد" for interpretation.
- "ما يزال غير مؤكد" for unresolved questions.

Do not create a raw research dump. Enrichment should deepen the note, not bury Mohamed under references.

## Research Budget

Default budget:

- Open the source's own references when present.
- Use 2 to 5 additional high-trust sources.
- Stop when the larger picture is clear enough for action or storage.

## Depth Selection

Choose the depth before drafting:

### Quick

Use only when Mohamed explicitly asks for speed or chat-only output. Do not create a heavy file unless requested.

### Standard

Use by default. Open source references where available and add 2 to 5 trustworthy context sources. This should answer the bigger picture without turning the task into a research project.

### Deep

Use when the topic affects money, investment, law, politics, health, security, reputation, or an active Mohamed system. Add more sources only when they materially improve the decision or reduce risk.

### Parked

Use when the source is interesting but low leverage. Capture the idea and explain why it should not become a project now.

## The Big Questions Checklist

Before writing, make sure the draft can answer the relevant questions:

- What is the core claim?
- Why does it matter now?
- Who is pushing it, funding it, benefiting from it, or harmed by it?
- How does the mechanism actually work?
- When did the key shift happen?
- Where is the action concentrated?
- What philosophy, incentive, or worldview is underneath?
- What is missing or weak in the source?
- What would a skeptical expert object to?
- What should Mohamed do next?

## Source Quality Ladder

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Original paper, filing, law, dataset, documentation, transcript, official statement.
2. Reputable institutional explanation or expert interview.
3. High-quality journalism with named sources and links.
4. Specialist blogs or technical writeups with evidence.
5. Commentary, social posts, and summaries.

If only weak sources are available, label the confidence.

## Required Enrichment Block

Before final drafting, maintain a short working block with:

```markdown
## Enrichment Brief

### Direct source claim

### Missing context found

### Counterpoints

### Mohamed application

### Confidence level

### Research not done
```

This block can remain internal or be integrated into the final note. If included in the final note, localize the heading to Arabic and avoid English mixed into Arabic lines.

## Anti-Bloat Rules

- Do not add every interesting historical detail.
- Do not turn every link into a literature review.
- Do not let enrichment delay file delivery if the user needs the note.
- If the topic deserves a separate deep dive, create a research-needed note or mention it in the idea-vault bridge.

## Verification

Before delivering, check:

- The note distinguishes source content from added research.
- The note answers what, why, who, how, when, where, and deeper philosophy when relevant.
- Weak or unresolved claims are labeled.
- Mohamed's next useful action is clear.
- The enrichment did not create a new project by accident.
