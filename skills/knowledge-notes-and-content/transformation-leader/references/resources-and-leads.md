Top 10 transformation failure modes and countermeasures:
1. Too many initiatives → 3 active max.
2. No quick win → first 90-day measured win.
3. Tool-first thinking → workflow/adoption first.
4. Hero-mode → build first pattern then delegate.
5. No owner handoff → named department owner required.
6. Vanity metrics → value ledger with run-rate.
7. Meeting scope creep → same-day state update.
8. Threatened departments → co-own and share credit.
9. IT/security late → partner early.
10. Over-promise → risks named early with corrective plan.
