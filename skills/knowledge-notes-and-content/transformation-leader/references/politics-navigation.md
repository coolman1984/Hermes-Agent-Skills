# Organizational Politics and Navigation

Transformations are political. Common patterns:
- Threatened department head: give co-ownership and visible credit for their win.
- IT/security gatekeeper: make partner early, before urgency.
- Peer who wants failure quietly: use results and executive air-cover, not confrontation.
- Credit question: share floor-level credit generously while value ledger states program wins plainly.

Every shipped win should create a champion who defends the program in rooms Mohamed is not in.

Use when: resistance is quiet, social, or status-driven. Backfires when: politics becomes manipulation instead of trust-building.
