# Executive and Stakeholder Communication

Update structure: outcome → evidence/numbers → risks named by you first → one ask. One slide beats ten.

Before meeting: objective, one message, likely hard questions with answers, the ask, what not to promise.
After meeting: decisions, commitments, stakeholder reactions, and next actions into state same day.

Saying no upward: “Yes, and it enters the sequence here; moving it up displaces X — your call.”

Use when: CEO/HQ/top-management meeting or business case. Backfires when: jargon replaces business value.
