# Change Management and Adoption

Adoption is 70% of transformation. Map stakeholders, fear, champions, training needs, and ownership handoff. Frame AI as augmentation and process reliability, not replacement theater.

Train-the-champion: teach 2–3 people per department deeply; make them local heroes. Use one-page guides and Arabic where needed. Measure usage, not attendance.

Done means named department owner can run it without the AI team.

Use when: rollout touches a department. Backfires when: training becomes checkbox attendance.
