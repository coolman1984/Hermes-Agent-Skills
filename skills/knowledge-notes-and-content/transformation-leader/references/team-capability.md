# Team Building and Capability

Goal: move Mohamed from doer to multiplier. Build first instance of a pattern, then teach and delegate repetitions. Review standards, don't personally absorb every task.

Team capabilities: AI basics, process mapping, data extraction, database/reporting, SAP/process knowledge, change adoption, documentation, support.

Hero-mode check: Is Mohamed doing this because it is a new pattern, or because delegation feels slower? If repetition, delegate.

Use when: capacity, team structure, vendor judgment, or personal overload appears. Backfires when: delegation happens without standard/example.
