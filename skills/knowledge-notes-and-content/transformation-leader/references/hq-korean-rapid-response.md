# HQ Korean Rapid Response

Use when Samsung HQ sends long Korean-language instructions, plans, or project mandates and Mohamed must understand, plan, and respond/extract actions within about one hour.

## Mission

Convert a Korean HQ document from overload into an executive-ready action brief: what HQ wants, why, deadlines, decisions, required local action, risks, and the one response/plan Mohamed should send or execute.

## 60-Minute Protocol

### 0–5 min — Capture and preserve source
- Save original file/message unchanged.
- Record sender, date, deadline, meeting/use case, and any attachments.
- Do not paraphrase before preserving source.

### 5–15 min — Translation for intent, not poetry
- Produce a literal working translation first.
- Mark uncertain terms, acronyms, factory-specific words, and HQ phrases that may carry political meaning.
- Separate: facts, asks, deadlines, metrics, implied expectations.

### 15–25 min — Mandate extraction
Extract into this structure:
- HQ objective.
- Required deliverables.
- Deadline and cadence.
- Metrics HQ likely cares about.
- What is mandatory vs suggested.
- What is ambiguous and needs clarification.
- Local departments affected.

### 25–40 min — Local impact and triage
Map each ask to:
- now / sequenced / clarify / push back diplomatically.
- owner department.
- dependency.
- risk.
- evidence needed.

If there are many asks, choose the one with highest deadline/executive exposure and park the rest in the intake log.

### 40–55 min — Produce the brief
Default deliverable:
1. one-paragraph summary.
2. action table.
3. risks/unknowns.
4. recommended next move.
5. draft reply or meeting message.

### 55–60 min — State update
- Add to `state/intake-log.md`.
- Add decisions/commitments to `state/decision-log.md`.
- If stakeholder signal appears, update `state/stakeholders.md`.

## Output Format

| HQ ask | Meaning | Local action | Owner | Deadline | Risk | Recommended response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Pushback rule

Do not silently accept impossible timelines. Use:

“Yes, we can start immediately. To protect quality, I recommend phase 1 by <date> covering <scope>. Expanding to <bigger scope> will displace <current priority> or require <resource/decision>.”

## Use when
- Long Korean HQ instructions arrive.
- Mohamed has less than two hours.
- There is CEO/HQ exposure.
- The document contains mixed strategy, tasks, dates, and implied pressure.

## Backfires when
- Used as a full legal translation.
- Used without preserving original wording.
- Ambiguous Korean terms are treated as certainty instead of flagged.
