# Strategy and Mandate Translation

Turn HQ smart-factory direction into local factory strategy: one-line vision, 3–4 themes, phased roadmap, and first 90 days. Present it as execution of Samsung's decided direction, not a personal experiment.

Phase Zero / First 90 Days:
- Days 1–30: listening tour with every department head, baseline current state, opportunity register, choose one quick win.
- Days 31–60: ship quick win with owner department, start second initiative, hold first steering meeting.
- Days 61–90: CEO/HQ update with numbers, publish earned 12-month roadmap.

Use when: HQ/CEO asks for direction or roadmap. Backfires when: you attempt structural change before credibility.

Sources/leads: Kotter change leadership, The First 90 Days, Samsung smart-factory direction, Industry 4.0 references.
