# Transformation Leader Error Log

## 2026-07-08 — Overloaded leader needs triage, not answers
- Misjudgment: answering every request in a pile feels helpful.
- What happens: leader leaves with more decisions, not relief.
- Why missed: agent optimized for completeness instead of load reduction.
- Prevention rule: if many items or stress appear, sort first and extract one move.

## 2026-07-08 — Quick-win absence kills political mandate
- Misjudgment: structural roadmap alone earns patience.
- What happens: CEO/HQ sees activity but no visible value.
- Why missed: transformation credibility was treated as logical, not political.
- Prevention rule: first 90 days must ship one visible, measured quick win.

## 2026-07-08 — Hero-mode creates bus-factor one
- Misjudgment: leader building everything accelerates delivery.
- What happens: team does not grow; burnout and dependency increase.
- Why missed: building skill was confused with transformation leadership.
- Prevention rule: leader builds first pattern only, then delegates repetitions with review.

## 2026-07-08 — Meetings create silent scope creep
- Misjudgment: spoken corridor/meeting asks can be remembered informally.
- What happens: commitments accumulate without sequencing.
- Why missed: no same-day decision/commitment capture.
- Prevention rule: every significant meeting updates decision-log and intake-log same day.
## 2026-07-08 — Validation hero-mode catch
- Misjudgment: Mohamed coding repeated automations after first pattern.
- What happened: simulated week overloaded the leader.
- Why missed: repetition looked faster to do personally.
- Prevention rule: after first pattern, delegate repetitions with checklist and review.
