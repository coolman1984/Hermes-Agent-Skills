# Weekly Intelligence Cycle

Use this reference when Mohamed says things like: "شغل الدورة" after the intelligence vault has been organized.

## Purpose
Turn a week of saved notes, media summaries, and idea cards into one practical decision instead of more storage.

## Inputs to inspect
- `CURRENT-SNAPSHOT.md` for the current strategic next step.
- `Projects/دورة الذكاء الأسبوعية.md` for the operating rules.
- `Ideas/Intelligence Vault/00 - Index.md` for available cards.
- `Ideas/Intelligence Vault/04 - Stronger Combinations.md` for combined opportunity lanes.
- `Ideas/Intelligence Vault/05 - Review Queue.md` for current candidates.
- Recent files under Downloads media-note roots when useful.
- The local wiki index if the decision should become durable knowledge.

## Default decision frame
Pick exactly three outputs:

1. Best near-money opportunity.
2. Best internal-system improvement.
3. One thing to park or stop for now.

Then choose only one execution experiment for the week.

## Good default output
For Mohamed, the chat summary should be short and decision-oriented:

- Decision of the week.
- Why this one.
- Internal improvement to preserve.
- What gets parked.
- Next concrete action.
- Files updated and verification status.

## Files to update
- Create a decision note under `Projects/` named like `قرار دورة الذكاء الأسبوعية - YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- Update `Ideas/Intelligence Vault/05 - Review Queue.md` with the selected experiment, internal improvement, parked item, and anti-distraction rule.
- Add a link from `Ideas/Intelligence Vault/00 - Index.md` if useful.
- If the output is durable knowledge, add a synthesis page under the local wiki and update its index/log.
- Update `CURRENT-SNAPSHOT.md` when the next operational step changes.
- Update `/root/tasks/todo.md` during execution.

## Verification
- Re-read the decision note and review queue.
- If the wiki was touched, run its validation and graph build.
- Confirm the snapshot next step matches the decision.

## Pitfalls
- Do not turn the cycle into a long report.
- Do not select more than one main experiment.
- Do not open a new project just because a note is interesting.
- Park interface/design ideas unless they support the selected experiment or a current decision bottleneck.
- Prefer a small sellable or system-strengthening move over broad ideation.
