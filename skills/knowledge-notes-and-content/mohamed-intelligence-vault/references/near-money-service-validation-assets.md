# Near-money service validation assets

Use this reference when an intelligence-vault idea has moved past raw ideation into a small sellable service experiment.

## Pattern captured from session

A raw opportunity was turned into a practical service test: reviewing sales-copy pages and writing sample audits. The useful move was not to build a brand, landing page, logo, or full product. The useful move was to create proof assets that can be shown to potential buyers.

## When to apply

Apply when Mohamed has an idea that is:

- close to money;
- service-shaped;
- testable with a small number of prospects;
- dependent on trust in Mohamed's taste, judgment, writing, research, or analysis;
- too early for automation or platform-building.

## Output shape

Create a project folder under the vault, not a new standalone system, for example:

```text
/root/Documents/Obsidian Vault/Projects/<اسم التجربة>/
```

Recommended files:

- `00 - فهرس.md` — what exists and how to use it.
- `01 - عينة ... .md` — one sample review per public example.
- `02 - عينة ... .md`
- `03 - عينة ... .md`
- `عرض الخدمة.md` — concise offer, scope, deliverables, and turnaround.

## Content standard

For each sample:

- preserve the source URL;
- state the page or offer being reviewed;
- give a practical judgment, not a generic critique;
- identify the promise, pain, objections, proof, and call to action;
- rewrite one important part when useful;
- keep Arabic clean and readable;
- avoid mixed English inside Arabic lines unless a term must remain in English on its own line.

## Decision rule

After creating 3 sample assets and one offer note, stop building and move to demand validation.

The next useful step is usually:

- identify 10 prospects with visible pages or offers;
- send a short message offering one small paid or trial review;
- treat replies as the signal.

## Pitfall

Do not let the idea-vault workflow become a writing workshop. Once there are enough proof assets, the task is demand testing, not more samples.
