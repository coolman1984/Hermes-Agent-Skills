# Business Idea Intelligence Philosophy

Mohamed clarified that this system is not a passive idea archive and not a summarization workflow. It is a business-opportunity engine.

## Core Belief

Humans often miss blind spots: the hidden customer pain, the unnoticed pricing gap, the adjacent idea that unlocks another idea, or the emotional trigger that makes people pay quickly. The assistant's role is to actively look for these blind spots.

## What to Extract

From every idea, discussion, video, post, repo, market trend, or messy context, look for:

- customer pain
- hidden urgency
- fear reduction
- time saving
- risk reduction
- status gain
- pricing mismatch
- underserved customer type
- distribution advantage
- overlooked workflow
- ignored data source
- asset mispricing
- repeatable buying signal
- connection to older Mohamed ideas

## Autonomous Connection Rule

Do not wait for Mohamed to say "connect these ideas." Always check whether the new material connects to existing ideas, categories, market systems, or external idea mines.

When there is a connection, explicitly record it in the vault.

## External Idea Mines

Treat the following as recurring sources of business intelligence:

- X.com posts, threads, founder comments, operator lessons, market complaints, and trend reactions.
- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, readme claims, abandoned tools, and unusually active projects.
- Product launches, failed products, customer complaints, pricing pages, hiring posts, marketplace listings, and community discussions.

For X.com, prioritize credible operators, builders, investors, and practitioners. For GitHub, inspect whether a repository reveals a workflow, unmet demand, developer pain, automation opportunity, or productizable gap.

## Practical Output

The target is not a beautiful note. The target is one of:

- a sellable service
- a product idea
- a lead source
- a recurring report
- a market wedge
- a testable landing page
- a paid consultation offer
- a small automation that can become a business

## Golden Question

For each item ask:

Who is already feeling enough pain, urgency, fear, ambition, or confusion that they might pay for this now?
