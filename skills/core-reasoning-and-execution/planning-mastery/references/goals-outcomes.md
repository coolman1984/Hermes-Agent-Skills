# Goals and Outcomes

Define objective before path. Outputs are things produced; outcomes are changed state, improved decision, reduced risk, or working capability. Success criteria must be observable and testable.

Frameworks: SMART for clarity, OKRs for alignment, North Star metric for product focus, working-backwards for end-state clarity. Limits: these can become ceremony for small tasks.

Worked example: “build an AI finance agent” becomes “within 4 weeks, produce a local agent that reads 3 finance files, answers 10 predefined questions with cited rows, refuses unsupported questions, and logs validation errors.”

Use when: ambition is vague or activity-shaped. Over-engineers when: task is tiny and success is obvious.
