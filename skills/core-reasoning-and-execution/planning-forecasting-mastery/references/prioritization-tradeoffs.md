# Prioritization and Trade-offs

Every plan sacrifices something. Choose deliberately using value, risk reduction, learning, urgency, dependency, cost of delay, and reversibility. Name what is not being done.

Tools: impact/effort, MoSCoW, RICE, cost of delay, reversible vs irreversible decisions.

Worked example: choose validation before report design because beautiful wrong reports destroy trust.

Invoke this when: too many tasks compete. Misleads when: scoring creates fake objectivity.

Sources/leads: product prioritization practice, lean product development, decision analysis.
