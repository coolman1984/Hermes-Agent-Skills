# Judgment and Forecasting

Good judgment separates knowns, assumptions, uncertainty, and update triggers. Use base rates, comparison classes, calibration, and frequent small forecasts. Forecasts must have a due date and resolution criterion.

Forecast record: claim, probability/range, evidence, assumptions, what would change my mind, review date.

Invoke this when: future outcome matters. Misleads when: forecast becomes theater and is never scored.

Sources/leads: Philip Tetlock Superforecasting, Good Judgment Project, Kahneman/Tversky planning fallacy.
