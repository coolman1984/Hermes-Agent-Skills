# Retrospective and Learning

Compare predicted vs actual. Ask: what did we expect, what happened, why the delta, what prevention rule improves next forecast?

Do not log every accident. Log reusable misses: wrong estimate class, missed dependency, wrong sequence, vague risk, hidden assumption.

Invoke this after: hard project, failed plan, or completed milestone. Misleads when: retrospective becomes blame.

Sources/leads: after-action reviews, agile retrospectives, forecasting calibration practice.
