# Risk Management

A useful risk is specific: event, trigger, impact, mitigation, owner, status. Use pre-mortem: imagine the project failed, then identify likely causes before starting.

Risk categories: data quality, dependency, integration, legal/security, performance, adoption, maintenance, timing.

Worked example: “supplier Excel header changes at month end” → trigger: missing required column; impact: wrong report; mitigation: schema validation gate and stop report.

Invoke this when: failure would cost money, time, trust, or safety. Misleads when: risk register is never reviewed.

Sources/leads: PMI risk management, Gary Klein pre-mortem, ISO 31000 concepts.
