# Feeds and Discovery

Use RSS/Atom, sitemap.xml, JSON-LD, microdata, search APIs, citation snowballing, related links, and incremental watermarks. Rank and dedupe by canonical URL, DOI, repo id, or content hash.

When to use: broad topic discovery. When it fails: noisy search results, duplicate mirrors, stale feeds.

Usage image: choose authorized source, collect raw, manifest it, then hand off.
