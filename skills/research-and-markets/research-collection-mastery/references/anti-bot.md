# Anti-Bot and Stop Signals

Use truthful User-Agent, pacing, caching, and sessions. Do not brute-force CAPTCHA, bypass paywalls, or evade explicit access restrictions. 403/429 means slow down, authenticate, or stop. Proxies are for legitimate network reliability or geo-authorized testing, not ToS evasion.

When to use: before any defended source. When it fails: source disallows automation; stop.

Usage image: choose authorized source, collect raw, manifest it, then hand off.
