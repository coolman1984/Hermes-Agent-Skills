# Andrej Karpathy

Andrej Karpathy is the originator of the LLM Wiki idea file that motivated this project. His core contribution here is not a productized tool, but a design pattern: use LLMs to maintain a persistent, interlinked markdown knowledge base rather than re-searching raw sources from scratch every time.

## Why he matters here

- He framed the wiki as a compounding artifact.
- He emphasized raw sources, a maintained wiki, and a schema file.
- He treated knowledge management as a bookkeeping problem that LLMs can help solve.
