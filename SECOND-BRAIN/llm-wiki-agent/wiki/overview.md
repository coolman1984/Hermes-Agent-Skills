# Overview

This wiki now has two connected clusters:

1. The original LLM Wiki pattern, which defines the mechanics of a persistent, compounding markdown knowledge base.
2. A new Egyptian-equity / EGX operating cluster, which turns official market facts into a risk-first investment doctrine.

## Current synthesis

The new EGX cluster says the same thing in several forms: start from risks, not stock names; assign each sector a clear function; keep official truth above interpretation; and treat cadence as part of the decision system. EGX, CBE, CAPMAS, and FRA are the truth layer. Reuters, TradingView, and Investing are follow/timing tools. Cash is acceptable when the clean basket is incomplete.

The strongest practical outcome is a portfolio doctrine that can be read in three layers: doctrine, source ladder, and operating cadence. The doctrine tells you what kind of exposure belongs in the portfolio. The ladder tells you which source has priority. The cadence tells you when to look and what to output.
